Everything here costs nothing. This is probably the single most useful page on this site — start
here before entering anything else, so you're not learning the vocabulary and competing for a
placement at the same time.

1. **[Free self-study stack](/opportunity/free-self-study-stack)** — CME Institute courses, YIS
   resources, and SEC/FINRA investor education, all free and self-paced. Not a credential, just
   the foundational knowledge everything else assumes you have. Start here.
2. **[Forage virtual job simulations](/opportunity/forage-virtual-job-simulations)** — free, 5–7
   hours per module, no application. Once you have the vocabulary, see what the actual work looks
   like at J.P. Morgan, Goldman Sachs, Citi, or Bloomberg.
3. **[CME Group Trading Challenge](/opportunity/cme-group-trading-challenge)** — free. Required
   CME Institute coursework plus a live one-week futures trading simulation on a professional
   platform. Recurring monthly, so there's always a next window.
4. **[Bloomberg Market Concepts / CFA Institute Investment Foundations](/opportunity/bloomberg-market-concepts-cfa-institute-investment-foundatio)**
   — **not yet verified: confirm current pricing before you start.** Sometimes free through a
   subscribing school or university, but our last check couldn't confirm current availability.
   Treat this as an optional add-on, not a first step, until it's confirmed.
