---
title: How we verify
description: Our methodology -- official sources only, an annual August audit, and how to report an error.
---

- **Official sources only.** Every fact on this site — deadlines, eligibility, cost — comes from
  the organizer's own website. We don't use aggregator blogs or forum posts, which are frequently
  wrong.
- **Every entry shows when it was last checked.** Each opportunity page displays a "last verified"
  date so you can see exactly how fresh that information is, rather than trusting a page that
  could be years stale.
- **We re-check every entry each August**, before fall deadlines open. This site's data was
  compiled and verified in August 2026.
- **When we can't fully confirm something, we say so instead of guessing.** If a program's terms
  changed, or an entry is a strategy suggestion rather than a real program, the page marks it as
  unverified with a specific note, rather than presenting it with the same confidence as
  everything else.
- **Dates change year to year.** Where we could only estimate a deadline from a prior year's
  cycle, the entry says so — treat those as "expect something similar," not a date to put in your
  calendar.
- **Found something wrong?** [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Ishaan-Kuruwa/GHIC-Opportunities-DB/issues)
  — anyone can, no account required beyond a free GitHub login. If you don't have one, email
  `[PLACEHOLDER CLUB EMAIL]` instead and we'll fix it in the next update.
- **The homepage's tile sizes aren't hand-picked.** A bigger tile means a higher prestige rating
  (already shown on every entry), or a confirmed deadline in the next 30 days — both computed
  straight from the data, the same way everything else on this page is.
