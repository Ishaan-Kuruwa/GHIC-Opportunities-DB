Not sure where to begin? Pick the situation that sounds like you.

## I'm a freshman

You have three or four years to build a track record. Start with things that don't require
experience yet, then layer on more later.

1. **[The Stock Market Game](/opportunity/the-stock-market-game)** — free in most states (funded
   for NC through the NC Council on Economic Education), open to grades 4–12, run through a
   teacher, and just 1–2 hours a week. The lowest-stakes way to start.
2. **[Wharton Global High School Investment Competition](/opportunity/wharton-global-high-school-investment-competition)**
   — free, open to grades 9–12, so a freshman is eligible from day one. Teams are 4–6 students
   from the same school, so join an upperclassman-led team rather than trying to start your own —
   you'll learn more from teammates who've done it before.
3. **[Forage virtual job simulations](/opportunity/forage-virtual-job-simulations)** — free, open
   to anyone, no application. Work through a self-paced module from J.P. Morgan, Goldman Sachs,
   Citi, or Bloomberg to see what the actual work looks like before you commit real time to
   competitions built around it.
4. **[DECA](/opportunity/deca)** — chapter membership open to grades 9–12. Join as a freshman and
   you get four years to build toward a national placement, instead of starting cold as a junior.
5. **[FBLA](/opportunity/fbla)** — same logic as DECA: chapter membership open to grades 9–12, and
   a four-year head start compounds. Check which one your school already has a chapter for.

## I have five hours a week

These fit a real weekly budget without crowding out everything else.

1. **[The Stock Market Game](/opportunity/the-stock-market-game)** — 1–2 hours a week, ongoing.
2. **[NC Capitol Challenge](/opportunity/nc-capitol-challenge)** — same time commitment (1–2 hours
   a week), same simulation, with the visibility of being paired with an NC General Assembly
   member.
3. **[Forage virtual job simulations](/opportunity/forage-virtual-job-simulations)** — each module
   runs 5–7 hours and is self-paced, so you can spread one out over a slower week.
4. **[CME Group Trading Challenge](/opportunity/cme-group-trading-challenge)** — about 5 hours,
   but over one week during its monthly windows, not an ongoing weekly commitment. High schoolers
   can enter but aren't eligible for prizes under CME's age terms — treat it as practice.

## I want quant

1. **[AMC 10/12 → AIME → USAMO](/opportunity/amc-10-12-aime-usamo)** — the pipeline underneath
   everything else here, sponsored in part by Jane Street, Citadel Securities, and Two Sigma.
   Usually a small fee covered by your school. Start this one earliest.
2. **[MathWorks Math Modeling (M3) Challenge](/opportunity/mathworks-math-modeling-challenge)** —
   free, a top-tier quant credential, teams of 3–5, one concentrated 14-hour window applying math
   to an unseen real-world problem.
3. **[Modeling the Future Challenge](/opportunity/modeling-the-future-challenge)** — free,
   individual or team, months of sustained project work in original actuarial research — for when
   you want something longer than a single weekend.
4. **[IMC Prosperity](/opportunity/imc-prosperity)** — free, a two-week Python-based algorithmic
   trading simulation. High schoolers can play but current terms restrict prizes to university
   students, so again: real practice, not a competition to win yet.
5. **[Jane Street Academy of Math and Programming](/opportunity/jane-street-academy-of-math-and-programming)**
   — free, plus a $5,000 scholarship, a 5-week residential program in New York. Specifically for
   graduating seniors who've faced barriers to advanced STEM access — the capstone, not the
   starting point.

## I want banking or asset management

1. **[Wharton Global High School Investment Competition](/opportunity/wharton-global-high-school-investment-competition)**
   — free, a 10-week team portfolio simulation, and the single most recognized flagship name in
   this list.
2. **[YIS Global Stock Pitch Competition](/opportunity/young-investors-society-global-stock-pitch-competition)**
   — $25 to enter, solo or a 2-person team. A written stock pitch plus a presentation is close to
   the actual interview format asset managers use.
3. **[Local RIA / community bank outreach](/opportunity/local-ria-community-bank-wealth-management-outreach)**
   — free, entirely self-directed cold outreach to firms near you. No brand name, but real work
   experience, which both colleges and employers weight more than most people expect.
4. **[Rock The Street, Wall Street](/opportunity/rock-the-street-wall-street)** — free, for
   female-identifying and non-binary students at a school that hosts a chapter. Direct mentorship
   from working finance professionals, plus an internship and job board built specifically for
   this program.

## I want entrepreneurship

1. **[Blue Ocean Student Entrepreneur Competition](/opportunity/blue-ocean-student-entrepreneur-competition)**
   — you need nothing but an idea: no prototype, no revenue, no existing business. Free, 10–20
   hours. The lowest-barrier way to find out if entrepreneurship competitions are for you.
2. **[NFTE World Series of Innovation](/opportunity/nfte-world-series-of-innovation)** — free,
   10–20 hours, built around sponsored challenge prompts tied to the UN Sustainable Development
   Goals.
3. **[Diamond Challenge](/opportunity/diamond-challenge)** — free to enter, teams of 2–4 plus an
   adult mentor, 3–4 months of sustained work. The premier high school entrepreneurship
   competition — the one to aim for once you're ready to commit a season.
4. **[Conrad Challenge](/opportunity/conrad-challenge)** — entry fees apply at later stages, 8
   months, heavily STEM-weighted. For students pairing entrepreneurship with a technical product
   idea.
5. **[LaunchX](/opportunity/launchx)** — paid, selective application, a multi-week full-time summer
   accelerator. For students ready to go deeper over the summer, if the cost works for your
   family.
